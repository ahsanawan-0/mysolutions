## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. Similar technologies, such as your browser's session storage or a script that measures visits, work in the same way. This policy covers all of them, and we call them "cookies" for short.

## How we use them

- **Strictly necessary** cookies make the site work and keep the contact form secure. They don't need your consent and can't be switched off here.
- **Optional** cookies, such as analytics, run only if you allow them. Privacy-friendly statistics that set no cookies and never identify you may run unless you opt out, as UK law permits; we say so clearly when that is the case.
- We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Content from other websites

We don't embed maps, videos or social media feeds that set their own cookies. Our contact page shows a map image stored on our own server; links to Google Maps, LinkedIn and other sites take you to those sites, where their own cookie policies apply. Our fonts and icons are also served from our own server.
