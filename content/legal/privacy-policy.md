## The information we collect

- **When you send an enquiry or ask for a quote:** your name, company, email address and phone number, your sector, the support you need, any tender deadline and preferred call-back time, your message, and whether you would like our email updates.
- **When you email or call us:** your contact details and whatever you choose to tell us.
- **When you become a client:** the information we need to do the work, such as tender documents, policies, evidence and details of the people involved that you share with us.
- **When you visit the website:** our hosting provider's servers record technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the site running and secure. To stop abuse of the contact form, we keep a scrambled (hashed) form of your IP address for one hour.
- **Analytics:** only if we use them and only as described in our [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/).

Please don't include health information or other sensitive details about individuals in an enquiry. If a project needs that kind of information, we will agree how to handle it with you first.

## How we use it and why

| What we do | Our lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote or call you back | Our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, or steps you ask us to take before a contract |
| Deliver our services and manage our working relationship | Performance of a contract |
| Send you insights and updates by email, if you tick the box on our form | Your consent, which you can withdraw at any time |

| Understand how the website is used | Your consent, or for privacy-friendly statistics our legitimate interests (see the [cookie policy](/cookie-policy/)) |
| Keep financial and business records | Legal obligation |

Every marketing email includes a simple way to unsubscribe.

## Your rights

You have the right to:

- ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you;
- ask us to correct anything inaccurate or incomplete;
- ask us to delete your data;
- ask us to restrict how we use it;
- object to how we use it, including at any time to direct marketing;
- ask us to send your data to you or another organisation in a portable format;
- withdraw your consent wherever we rely on it.

To use any of these rights, email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}). There is usually no fee. We will reply within one month, or tell you within that month if a complex request needs up to two more.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, email [{{email}}](mailto:{{email}}) with "Data protection complaint" in the subject line. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days, look into it and tell you the outcome without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/) or on 0303 123 1113.
