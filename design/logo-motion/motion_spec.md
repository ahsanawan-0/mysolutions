## 2. Geometry (Phase 2 — VECTOR)

- **Complexity ladder level 1 (primitives):** the mark is exactly three polygons with the coordinates from PROMPTS.md P07 (≈ 97–99 % pixel overlap measured when the prompt was written). No tracing was run.
- **Wordmark:** the docs guessed *Saira Condensed*; a pixel fit of candidate fonts at the measured cap height (`scripts/logo.mjs`, least-squares letter-spacing against the 11 glyph columns) ranked **Khand** (OFL, Indian Type Foundry) far ahead: Khand Regular IoU **0.980**, ink-weight ratio **1.00**, column residual 0.3 px for "My Solutionz"; Khand Medium IoU **0.983** for the tagline (letter-spacing +1.46 px at 83.5 px). Saira Condensed scored 0.32. Letters are outlined to paths (exact letterforms; one `<path class="letter">` each so they can cascade).
- **Overlay QA** (`render_overlay.py`, headless Chrome, whole lockup placed in the source frame): vertical lockup **IoU 0.992** (src-only 1 281 px, render-only 569 px of 1563²); horizontal lockup IoU 0.978. `outputs/fit_iterations/01_mark_polygons_khand_overlay.png` is uniformly tinted with no uncovered source and no fringes → accepted at iteration 1. Smoothness gate: polygons and font outlines only, no traced knots. `outputs/overlay_progress_strip.png`.
- Residuals: JPEG anti-aliasing and the hinting of the original raster; none structural.

## 3. Choreography (Phase 3 — MOTION)

Shape: anticipation 150–350 ms (12.5 %) : action 350–1 120 ms (48 %) : follow-through 1 010–1 600 ms (37 %) — the follow-through overlaps the navy bar's landing so no part stops alone. Drag hierarchy: streak (staging) → hexagon (root) → azure bar → navy bar (heavier, +120 ms, same duration) → letters → tagline.

| Phase | Time (ms) | Part | Motion | Pattern | Easing (literal in keyframes) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Staging | 0–450 | `#streak` | Soft azure band on the 120° diagonal (rising to the right) slides across the empty canvas, opacity 0 → .45 → 0 | Staging hold + secondary action | enter |
| Anticipation | 150–350 | `#hex-sky` | Fades in at scale .6 and coils to .55 | Scale-Pop (coil) | enter |
| Action | 350–850 | `#hex-sky` | Pops to 1.02 (700) then settles to 1.0 | Scale-Pop with overshoot | enter → elegant settle |
| Action | 420–1 000 | `#bar-azure` | Grows up its own 60° diagonal: clip-path band perpendicular to the axis sweeps bottom-right → top-left, plus a 24 px drift along the axis | Mask Wipe | enter |
| Action | 540–1 120 | `#bar-navy` | Same, staggered +120 ms | Mask Wipe | enter |
| Follow-through | 1 010–1 530 | `.letter` × 11 | translateY(125 px = 0.6 em) → 0 + fade, 240 ms each, **28 ms stagger** (12 % of a letter) | Letter Cascade | enter |
| Follow-through | 1 260–1 600 | `#tagline` | Left → right inset wipe with a 6 px drift | Mask Wipe | settle |
| Settle | 1 600 | all | Exactly the static logo | Final Frame Contract | — |

Deviation from docs/07 §3: the doc's "280 ms letters, 40 ms stagger" cannot fit 11 letters into 1 000–1 350; 240/28 keeps the cascade inside the 1 600 ms total (last letter lands at 1 530). No two parts share a start or an end frame (letters start at 1 010 so none coincides with the azure bar's landing at 1 000).

Tokens: `--p2m-duration: 1600ms`, `--p2m-ease-enter`, `--p2m-ease-settle`, `--p2m-ease-elegant`, `--p2m-overshoot: 1.02`, `--p2m-squash: 0` (documentation + shorthands; keyframes carry the literal curves). Every animation uses `fill-mode: both`; the streak ends at opacity 0.

Principles applied: staging (streak → mark → wordmark → tagline reading order), anticipation (coil), slow in/slow out (ease-out entrances, elegant settle), follow-through & overlapping action (bar stagger, letter cascade), timing (heavier navy bar starts later over the same duration), exaggeration (1.02 only on the root part), solid drawing (uniform scale, clip wipes never deform geometry), appeal (the "M" literally builds upward, which is the brand story).

Atomic motions in the showcase (generic script atoms on the same SVG): hover lift, breathing pulse, arc swing, press. On the site the hover is the **bar re-draw** at 2× speed and the loading state is the **breathing** loop (`logo.css`).

## 4. Motion QA (evidence in `outputs/`)

- **Frames** `motion_frames/frame_*.png` at 0, 150, 350, 450, 600, 700, 850, 1 000, 1 120, 1 250, 1 400, 1 530, 1 600 ms → `motion_strip.png`. Inspected: the streak stages, the hexagon reads as a coil before the pop, the bars show a clean perpendicular cut mid-wipe (t = 450 azure tip, t = 600 both), letters cascade rather than lockstep, nothing clips the viewBox.
- **Easing probe** `easing_probe.json`: `#hex-sky` scale at t = 500 → 0.917 (linear would be 0.751); `#bar-azure` drift at t = 600 → 4.1 px (linear 8.3); `#letter-1` at t = 1 130 → 20 px (linear 62.5). All keyframe easings are applied.
- **Ink-delta sweeps** `ink_sweep_bars.json` (400–1 140, 20 ms) and `ink_sweep_text.json` (1 000–1 600): no stall-then-jump signature; the only low deltas are the ease-out tails before the next part starts.
- **Final Frame Contract:** same-pipeline `?static=1` vs `?t=1600` (Playwright, 900×700, DPR 2) → **0 differing pixels**. Cross-pipeline diff against `final_render.png` (sharp) mean 1.7 / 2.6 % pixels ≥ 25 = resampling noise, confirmed visually. `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` renders the static logo immediately (`final_contract_reduced_motion.png`).
