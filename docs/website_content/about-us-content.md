## 3. Our Approach `[#our-approach]`
**Heading:** A Practical, Strategic Approach to Tender Writing and Bid Management

At My Solutionz, we believe a strong tender submission starts with a thorough understanding of the business behind it. Every contract has different requirements, and every client brings their own experience, capabilities and strengths. Our approach to tender writing and bid management focuses on bringing these elements together to develop clear, compliant and competitive bids.

We combine practical business knowledge, sector-specific expertise and a structured bid management process to support our clients from the initial tender assessment through to final submission. Rather than relying on standard templates or broad claims, we build each response around the buyer's requirements, relevant supporting evidence and the client's ability to deliver.

**01. Understanding Your Business**
Before beginning any tender response, we take time to understand your organisation, services, operational processes, workforce, experience and commercial objectives. This allows us to identify your competitive strengths and gather the information needed to present your business accurately.
Whether you operate in healthcare, transport, cleaning, security, education or facilities management, we approach each tender with an understanding of the particular standards and expectations within your sector.

**02. Tender Analysis & Bid Strategy**
We carefully review the invitation to tender (ITT), service specification, evaluation criteria, contractual requirements and submission instructions. This helps us identify mandatory requirements, potential risks, scoring priorities and areas where your business can demonstrate added value.
Using these findings, we develop a clear bid strategy, establish response priorities and plan how best to address the contracting authority's expectations.

**03. Evidence-Led Tender Writing**
Our professional tender writers develop responses that are specific to your business and the contract you are pursuing. We focus on explaining how your services will be delivered, the resources available, how quality will be monitored and how contractual outcomes will be achieved.
We draw on relevant operational evidence, previous contract experience, policies and working practices to produce persuasive, well-structured responses that directly address the evaluation questions.

**04. Quality Review & Compliance Checks**
Before finalising a submission, we review each response against the tender specification and scoring criteria. We check that questions have been fully addressed, supporting evidence is consistent, word limits have been respected and the required documents are accounted for.
Our tender review process also identifies unclear statements, unsupported claims and gaps that could affect the quality or compliance of the submission.

**05. Final Submission & Ongoing Support**
We support clients through the final stages of the bidding process, including document preparation, submission checks and uploading tender responses where agreed.
Our involvement does not have to end with one tender. We also provide ongoing bid management, tender opportunity searches and strategic support to help businesses build a more consistent approach to securing public and private sector contracts.

**Closing heading:** Every Bid Deserves a Well-Considered Approach
We recognise the time, resources and commercial importance involved in preparing a tender. That is why we treat every submission with the attention it deserves, combining detailed preparation, open communication and a clear understanding of what the buyer is asking for.
Our aim is simple: to help you submit bids that represent your business at its best and give you a stronger basis to compete.
