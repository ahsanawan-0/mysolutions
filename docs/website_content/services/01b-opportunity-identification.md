### [Text] Understanding Your Business *Comes First*
**Sub:** The Right Opportunity Starts with Knowing What Your Business Can Deliver

Before searching for contracts, we take time to understand your organisation and what makes it competitive.

We consider your business size, industry experience, geographical coverage, workforce capacity, financial position, accreditations, previous contract performance and operational capabilities. Equally important are your commercial objectives, whether you want to enter the public sector market, expand into new locations, increase contract values or build a more consistent pipeline of opportunities.

This understanding allows us to establish a tailored opportunity profile, identifying the types of contracts that fit your business rather than presenting every tender available within your industry.

Our objective is straightforward: **to find opportunities that make commercial and operational sense for your organisation.**

---

### [Steps] How Our Tender Opportunity *Identification Works*
**Sub:** A Targeted Search and Assessment Process

We follow a structured approach to finding, reviewing and shortlisting relevant contract opportunities.

**01. Business Profiling & Opportunity Criteria** (`user-focus`)
We begin by reviewing your organisation's services, strengths, resources and growth plans. From this information, we establish suitable contract categories, geographical preferences, contract values, service requirements and other relevant selection criteria.

**02. Targeted Tender Opportunity Searches** (`binoculars`)
We search relevant UK procurement portals, public sector contract notices, framework opportunities and other appropriate tender sources to identify contracts matching your business profile.
*(detail paragraph)* Our searches focus on opportunities relevant to your industry, delivery capabilities and preferred markets, rather than producing lengthy lists of unrelated tenders.

**03. Tender Screening & Requirement Analysis** (`file-magnifying-glass`)
Finding an opportunity is only the first step. We examine the available tender notice and procurement documentation to understand the contract scope, eligibility conditions, technical requirements, financial thresholds, accreditations, experience criteria, evaluation methodology and submission deadlines.
*(detail paragraph)* This helps us identify requirements that your organisation can meet, potential barriers to participation and any commitments that may affect your decision to bid.

**04. Compatibility Assessment & Shortlisting** (`list-checks`)
We compare the contract requirements against your organisation's capabilities and commercial objectives.
*(detail paragraphs)* Our assessment considers whether your business meets the eligibility requirements, possesses relevant experience, has sufficient delivery capacity and can present a credible competitive offer.
We then shortlist suitable opportunities, highlighting their strengths, potential challenges and overall relevance to your business.

**05. Opportunity Presentation & Recommendations** (`presentation-chart`)
We present the selected opportunities with a clear summary of the contract, buyer, estimated value where available, location, key requirements, submission deadline and our assessment of suitability.
*(detail paragraph)* You receive practical recommendations on which opportunities are worth exploring further, helping you decide where to invest your bidding resources.

### [List] Tender *Compatibility Analysis*
**Sub:** Assessing Your Fit Before You Commit to Bidding

A relevant tender is not necessarily a suitable tender. Understanding the difference can save valuable resources and improve the quality of your bidding decisions.

Our compatibility analysis examines the factors that influence your organisation's ability to compete and deliver:

| Icon | Title | Text |
|---|---|---|
| `seal-check` | Eligibility & Compliance | Whether your organisation meets mandatory participation requirements, qualifications, accreditations and financial thresholds. |
| `briefcase` | Experience & Technical Capability | How closely your previous contracts, industry expertise and service capabilities match the buyer's expectations. |
| `map-trifold` | Operational & Geographical Fit | Whether you have the workforce, resources, service coverage and capacity required to fulfil the contract. |
| `currency-gbp` | Commercial Suitability | Whether the contract value, duration, delivery obligations and potential costs align with your business objectives. |
| `ranking` | Competitive Position | How effectively your organisation could demonstrate its strengths against the published evaluation criteria and likely demands of the contract. |

Based on these findings, we help you distinguish promising opportunities from those that may be unsuitable, supporting a more informed **Bid/No-Bid decision**.
